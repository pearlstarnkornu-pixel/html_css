Welcome

The Copper Ladle has been serving hearty, homestyle meals to the Millbrook community since 2014. Every dish on our menu is made from scratch using ingredients sourced from farms within thirty miles of our kitchen. Whether you're stopping in for a quick lunch or settling in for a long dinner with friends, our goal is simple: good food, generous portions, and a warm welcome.

Menu Highlights

A few of the dishes our regulars order again and again:

Menu Notes

Braised Short Rib
Beef short rib slow-cooked for six hours until it falls off the bone, served over root vegetables.
Wild Mushroom Risotto
Creamy arborio rice simmered with a blend of wild mushrooms, finished tableside with parmesan.
Pan-Seared Trout
Locally caught trout, pan-seared to a crisp skin and topped with a bright lemon butter sauce.

About Us

The Copper Ladle was founded by chef Marion Ellery, who spent a decade cooking in restaurant kitchens before opening a place of her own. The name comes from the copper ladle her grandmother used for family soup nights, which still hangs above our kitchen pass today. We believe a meal should feel like it was made by someone who cares, not assembled on a line.
