Latest from the Trail

This week we covered the ridge line above Copper Basin, where the wildflowers are finally in full bloom. The trail climbs steadily for the first two miles before opening onto a wide alpine meadow with views of three separate peaks.

Pack layers — mornings are still cold at elevation, but the afternoon sun on the exposed switchbacks can be surprisingly intense. Water sources are scarce after the first creek crossing, so plan accordingly.
