    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is SimpleSite?

    SimpleSite is an online platform designed to help beginners learn HTML and CSS in a simple and effective way. We provide comprehensive courses, tutorials, and resources to guide you through your web development journey.

    How do I get started?

    Getting started with SimpleSite is easy! Simply sign up for an account, choose a course that interests you, and begin learning at your own pace.

    What are the prerequisites for the courses?

    There are no prerequisites for our courses. We start from the basics and gradually build up your skills.

    How long does it take to complete a course?

    The duration of each course varies depending on the complexity and the amount of time you dedicate to it. On average, most courses can be completed within a few weeks.

    Do you offer certificates upon completion?

    Yes, we provide certificates of completion for all our courses. These certificates can be shared on your LinkedIn profile or added to your resume.

    Is there a community for learners?

    Yes, we have a vibrant community of learners and instructors. You can join our forums, participate in discussions, and get support from fellow students and experts.
