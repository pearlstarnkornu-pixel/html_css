Classes We Offer

Whether you have never touched clay before or you are refining a technique you already know, our studio runs small classes capped at eight students so everyone gets time at the wheel.

Beginner Wheel Throwing

A six-week course covering centering, pulling walls, and trimming your first bowls and mugs. No experience required.

Glaze Chemistry Workshop

A weekend intensive for returning students who want to mix their own glazes instead of relying on pre-made buckets.

From the Studio Floor

Our students' work ranges from rustic stoneware to delicate porcelain. A rotating selection is on display in the front window, and pieces are available for purchase at the front desk.
