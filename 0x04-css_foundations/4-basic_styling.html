Learning to Slow Down on the Ridge Trail

Posted on March 3rd · Filed under Reflections

The first mile always feels like an argument with my own legs. By the second mile, the trail stops asking anything of me and I finally notice the wind moving through the grass instead of the ache in my knees. There is a specific kind of quiet that only shows up after you have walked long enough to stop narrating the walk to yourself.

I used to bring headphones. Now I bring a notebook instead, and most days I do not even open it. Just knowing it is there, in case a sentence worth keeping shows up, is enough.
