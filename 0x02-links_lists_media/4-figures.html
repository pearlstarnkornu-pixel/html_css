Using Figure and Figcaption

This page shows images grouped with their captions using <figure> and <figcaption>, which keeps an image and its explanation tied together as one unit of content.

Why Use Figures Instead of Plain Images?

A plain <img> tag has no built-in way to attach a caption; any nearby text is just a separate paragraph with no guaranteed relationship to the image. Wrapping the image and its caption in a <figure> element makes that relationship explicit in the page's structure, which helps screen readers announce the caption as belonging to that specific image and lets the whole figure be moved, styled, or referenced as one self-contained unit.
