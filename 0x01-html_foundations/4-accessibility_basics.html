Riverside Community Garden Guide

Everything you need to know before renting a plot this season.

Getting a Plot

Plots are assigned on a first-come, first-served basis starting each March. New members should fill out the plot application form to join the waiting list.

What Counts as a Standard Plot

A standard plot measures 3 meters by 3 meters, enough space for a mix of vegetables, herbs, and a couple of small fruit bushes.

Tools and Shared Resources

The tool shed near the north gate holds shovels, wheelbarrows, and watering cans that any plot holder can borrow during opening hours.

Water Access

Each row of plots has its own spigot. Members are asked to water in the early morning or evening to reduce evaporation and keep water pressure fair for everyone.

Questions and Support

For anything not covered here, read the full garden handbook or speak with the plot coordinator on Saturday mornings.
