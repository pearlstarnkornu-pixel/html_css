Why Typography Matters

Good typography is invisible when it works, and exhausting when it doesn't. Font choice, size, weight, and spacing all combine to make a page either effortless or tiring to read. Wrapping Up

Combining a readable font family, a sensible line-height, appropriate font sizes and weights for hierarchy, and deliberate use of color gives even a plain page a professional, intentional feel — no images or layout tricks required.
