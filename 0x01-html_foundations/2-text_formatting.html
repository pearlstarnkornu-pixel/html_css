Trail Runner 3000 Hiking Boots — Review Notes

After six months on rocky trails, the ankle support is still the standout feature of these boots — it genuinely prevented a twisted ankle on a loose scree slope. The upper fabric is surprisingly breathable for something rated waterproof, which was not what I expected going in.

Update, March 2026: the manufacturer has confirmed a recall on laces produced before batch 4021. If your pair matches that batch, contact support for a free replacement set.

The listed weight was originally 1.4 kg per pair 1.6 kg per pair after the company corrected an error on their spec sheet last year.

Sizing runs about half a size small, so if you normally wear a 91/2 in most brands, order a size 10 here. The rubber outsole is built on a compound the company calls H2Grip, designed to stay tacky on wet rock.

Note: this review reflects personal use and is not sponsored by the manufacturer.
